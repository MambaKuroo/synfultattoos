ARTWORK

MULTIMEDIA ARTIST


Sydney is a versatile multimedia artist with a background spanning visual development, digital animation, and traditional studio art. Specializing in concept design, character creation, and 2D rigging, Sydney bridges creative storytelling with technical execution. From crafting intuitive UI/UX design systems to producing physical studio media, Sydney brings a highly adaptable, multi-disciplinary approach to every project.
Equipped with a diverse digital toolkit, Sydney regularly works with industry-standard software, including Clip Studio Paint, Procreate, Live2D, Blender, and Visual Studio Code. Whether designing asset pipelines for indie projects or shaping character concepts from initial sketch to final rig, Sydney combines technical precision with a dynamic artistic vision.
