A LITTLE ABOUT ME


Pronouns: She/Her

Hi, I’m Sydney Luck — an actor and performance enthusiast rooted in Central Florida. For me, acting is all about genuine connection: connecting with the story, the character, and the receiver. I’m particularly drawn to game characters, dramas, traumas, comedies, or indie shorts. I bring a strong work ethic, high energy, and an open, adaptable mindset to every recording, rehearsal, and set. I also have a terrible array of dad jokes that will come at the worst moments, credits to my own father.


Some important factors to note: I am prepared to travel to out of state locations , however, as long as I have sufficient advance notice. For film or visuals, I currently have many tattoos, though they can easily be covered with the right attire or makeup.

A little more cool stuff about me: I am an Artist! I am commissionable for multiple medium projects, such as Digital Art, Animation, Character Creation, 3D Modeling, various UI coding, and website coding - much like this very one you're visiting now! I am also a content creator on various platforms!

THE REAL WHY

I'm a creative - not just 'I draw', no I create beyond the paper. New people, new ideas. I build, I learn, I craft, and I make possible. And if it's something I don't know how to do, I teach myself how to. I'm a restless soul. I can't be doing nothing - it would drive me mad if I didn't use my time I have living for creating and experiences.
I want to be someone that's remembered, not awarded. I don't need anyone to tell me I 'did a good job', I want people to be affected by me in a way. To influence others to be creative - to turn myself into a motivation for the restless.
